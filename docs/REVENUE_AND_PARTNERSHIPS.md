# SkillSwap — additional revenue and partnerships (proposed, not integrated)

1. **Institution partnerships**: collaborate with colleges, ITIs and local coaching centres to offer skill labs, placement workshops and co-branded assessments. Start with pilot seats and revenue-share agreements.
2. **Team training**: sell employer-funded bundles of technical/digital skills with designated mentors and reporting, priced by seat or cohort.
3. **Certified learning tracks**: offer optional paid assessment and verified completion certificates, clearly distinguishing SkillSwap completion from any officially accredited degree.
4. **Premium mentor storefronts**: optional monthly subscription for advanced scheduling, analytics, course merchandising and branded pages; ranking should never misrepresent learner ratings.
5. **Project review services**: learners can pay for a code review, CV review, portfolio consultation or mock interview, with a transparent 5% base commission policy or separately published fees.
6. **Group cohort learning**: paid cohort events and employer-sponsored challenges with clear cancellation and attendance policies. Current group events are course-enrollment-gated; a paid per-seat registration system would require additional checkout logic.
7. **Affiliate partnerships**: carefully disclosed partnerships with books, learning equipment, labs and cloud education credits; never hide paid placement in unbiased rankings.

Before launching any of these: design consent, quality controls, fee/refund terms, taxation and separate settlement reporting.
